# 🎵 SMY-02 力量反转

> 四梦归一 · 第二幕主题曲（第31-54回）

## 场景
四界融合加速，力量对比发生反转。文人开始拥有武力（诸葛亮会飞），武将获得智慧（张飞会写诗），妖精变得善良，神仙变得脆弱。旧规则全部失效。

## Suno Style Prompt
Electronic rock with unstable beats that gradually stabilize, glitchy transitions, male and female mixed vocals representing merged worlds, synthesizers and Chinese instruments dueling then cooperating, BPM fluctuates then locks in, 100 BPM settling, B minor to B major, sounds like a system rebooting

## Tags
electronic-rock,glitch,B-minor-to-major,system-reboot,merged-worlds,unstable-beats

## 创作笔记
- **节拍不稳定→稳定**：模拟规则从崩溃到重建
- **角色反转**：每个角色都获得了"不属于自己世界"的能力——这正是融合的意义
- **B minor→B major**：从小调转大调——混乱中发现新秩序
