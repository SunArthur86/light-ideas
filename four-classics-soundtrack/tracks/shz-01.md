# 🎵 SHZ-01 替天行道·序曲

> 水浒传 · 开篇 · 乱自上作

## 场景
北宋末年，天下腐败。一百零八个好汉被逼上梁山，打出"替天行道"的大旗。他们是被体制抛弃的人——武者、小吏、渔民、猎户——却组成了一支义军。

## Suno Style Prompt

Wild Chinese rock with electric guitar and suona, aggressive drums and folk instrumentation, raw male vocals with rebellious energy, mix of rock attitude and Chinese folk music, starts explosive, strips back for verse, full blast chorus, suona and guitar dual solo in bridge, 120 BPM, E minor, the sound of outlaws with a conscience

## Tags

chinese-rock,suona,electric-guitar,aggressive-drums,male-vocal,rebellious,E-minor,outlaw,folk-rock,wild

## 创作笔记

- **唢呐+电吉他**：唢呐是"最中国的"民间乐器，电吉他是最反叛的——两者结合=水浒精神
- **120 BPM**：快节奏——造反不是慢慢来的事
- **"行的是一颗心"**：结局虽然悲剧，但过程中的义气是真实的
